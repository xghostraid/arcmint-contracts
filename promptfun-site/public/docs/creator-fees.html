Creator fees

Get your pump.fun creator fees

When you launch through promptfun.fun on Solana, pump.fun assigns creator fees on trades. Those fees go to the wallet you set at launch. promptfun.fun takes 0% — creators receive 100% of the pump.fun creator-fee share that goes to your recipient.

Three steps

  1. Easiest: tell Claude in chat

    When you ask to launch a coin, include your Solana wallet in plain language. Claude sets creatorWallet on prepare_launch before you tap Launch it.

    Example prompt: “Launch MOON on mainnet — send creator fees to my wallet: …” (paste your full address).

    A Solana address is usually 43–44 characters: letters and numbers only, no spaces. Example shape: Ab1c…Xy9z — yours will be one continuous string.

  2. Claim wallet (email)

    Go to promptfun.fun/claim. Sign in with the same email you use in Claude. We create or look up a user-owned Solana payout wallet for locked creator fees.

    This path needs Privy configured on the promptfun.fun server. If claim is unavailable, use your own external wallet in chat instead.

  3. Always set a wallet

    Warning: If you launch without a wallet, creator fees can lock to the default server recipient — not you. Give an address in chat or set up claim before you confirm the launch.

What you get

  • 100% of pump.fun creator fees that pump.fun routes to your launch fee recipient (creatorWallet).
  • 0% promptfun.fun fee on those creator fees.
  • Fees follow pump.fun’s own rules (lock timing, distribution, and amounts are set by pump.fun).
