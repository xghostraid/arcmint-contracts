Creator fees

Your pump.fun share — Sunny simple

promptfun.fun does not add a platform tax on launches. Creator fees are whatever pump.fun assigns to your coin’s fee recipients — we just help you lock them to creatorWallet at launch.

Today (before $PROMPT mint)

100% to you Every launch sends the full pump.fun creator-fee share to your fee recipient. promptfun.fun keeps 0% platform cut. No 50/50 split is applied on-chain until the token policy is live.

After $PROMPT launches

Unlocks with $PROMPT Same week as our X product post and the mint going live. At prepare_launch, we read your creatorWallet balance on chain:

Holder tier

100%

≥ 1 whole $PROMPT in creatorWallet

or

Default

50%

creator · 50% promptfun.fun treasury

Splits apply to pump.fun creator-fee share only — not network fees (sponsor still pays those on mainnet) and not an extra promptfun markup.

Which wallet counts?

  • creatorWallet you pass at launch, or
  • Your signed-in claim-later Solana wallet when OAuth + Privy are enabled and you omit creatorWallet.

Network fees vs creator fees

The launch preview shows network fees (Solana rent and compute). On sponsored mainnet, promptfun pays those from the sponsor wallet. Creator fees accrue from trading on pump.fun and land when fees are distributed on chain.

Policy effective when $PROMPT mint is live and promptfun enables tier checks in production. Questions? See the FAQ or home creator-fees beat.
