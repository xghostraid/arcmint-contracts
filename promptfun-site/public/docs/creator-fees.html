Creator fees

Creator fees & $PROMPT tiers

Launch on pump.fun through promptfun.fun and earn SOL from trades. Set your payout wallet at launch. promptfun.fun takes 0% platform cut — the tiers below split pump.fun’s creator-fee share only (not network fees).

Set your payout wallet

  1. Tell Claude in chat

    Include your Solana wallet when you launch. Claude sets creatorWallet on prepare_launch before you tap Launch it.

    Example: “Launch MOON on mainnet — send creator fees to my wallet: …”

  2. Claim wallet (email)

    promptfun.fun/claim — sign in with the same email as Claude for a user-owned Solana payout wallet when Privy is enabled.

Fee tiers at launch

Unlocks with $PROMPT We read your creatorWallet when you launch:

Non-holders split pump.fun creator-fee share only — not an extra promptfun markup.

Which wallet counts?

  • creatorWallet you pass at launch, or
  • Your signed-in claim-later Solana wallet when OAuth + Privy are enabled and you omit creatorWallet.

Network fees vs creator fees

The launch preview shows network fees (Solana rent and compute). On sponsored mainnet, promptfun.fun pays those from the sponsor wallet. Creator fees accrue from trading on pump.fun.
