How it works

Four steps. You’re in charge of the one that matters.

  1. 01

    Say it

    Tell Claude what you want done, in your own words. Claude turns it into a clear request for promptfun.fun.

  2. 02

    See the preview

    promptfun.fun checks the request and builds the exact transaction. Then it reads it back to you in plain language: what happens, on which network, and what it costs.

  3. 03

    Approve in your wallet

    Open the approval link and confirm in your own wallet. Nothing happens until you tap approve, and you can always say no.

  4. 04

    Get the receipt

    promptfun.fun looks the transaction up on the chain and checks it matches what you approved. Claude only says “done” once the chain confirms it.

Safety

Your wallet, your call. Every time.

We never hold your keys

promptfun.fun builds the transaction. Your wallet signs and sends it. We don’t store keys, seed phrases or funds, ever.

Nothing faked

No made-up results and no pretend success. A receipt only says “confirmed” after we read the transaction back from the chain.

You see what you sign

The preview is read from the transaction itself, not from Claude’s summary. What you approve is what goes onchain.

Claude can’t approve for you

Claude can ask for an action, but only you can approve it, in your own wallet. Each approval covers one action and nothing else.

FAQ

Before you plug it into Claude.

What is promptfun.fun?

It’s a connector for Claude that turns what you ask for into a real onchain action. It prepares the transaction, shows you a plain-language preview, lets you approve it in your own wallet and hands back a receipt checked against the chain.

Do I need to know about crypto?

No. You need a Claude account and a wallet for the chain you’re using. promptfun.fun explains each action in plain words before you approve it.

Is it live?

Not yet. Token launches on Solana are being tested on devnet, which uses test tokens with no real value. pump.fun launches and EVM chains are coming. This page says when each one opens.

Which chains does it support?

Solana comes first. EVM chains such as Base and Ethereum come next. The Chains section above shows the current state of each one.

Does promptfun.fun hold my money or keys?

No. Your wallet signs and sends every transaction. promptfun.fun never sees your keys or seed phrase and never holds your funds.

Can Claude do things without asking me?

No. Claude can prepare an action, but nothing happens onchain until you approve it in your wallet. If you reject it, nothing is sent.

What does it cost?

Each chain charges a small network fee. Your wallet pays it, and the preview shows it before you approve. We’ll publish any promptfun.fun fee here before launch.

Which Claude plans work?

Any plan that lets you add a custom connector. promptfun.fun only needs one.

What if something goes wrong?

You get the real status. If the transaction failed, you rejected it or the approval link expired, Claude tells you that. It never says something worked when it didn’t.
