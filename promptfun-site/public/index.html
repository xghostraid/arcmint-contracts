How it works

Four steps, all inside ChatGPT. You’re in charge of the one that matters.

  1. 1

    Say it

    Tell ChatGPT what you want done, in your own words. ChatGPT turns it into a clear request for promptfun.fun.

  2. 2

    See the preview

    promptfun.fun checks the request and builds the exact transaction. Then it reads it back to you in plain language: what happens, on which network, and what it costs.

  3. 3

    Confirm in chat

    Confirm right in ChatGPT. For a launch, promptfun.fun pays the network fee, so you don’t need a wallet. Nothing happens until you confirm, and you can always say no. Prefer your own wallet? Approve in it instead.

    Being built
  4. 4

    Get the receipt

    promptfun.fun looks the transaction up on the chain and checks it matches what you confirmed. The receipt shows up in the same chat, and ChatGPT only says “done” once the chain confirms it.

Connect to ChatGPT

Add it once. Then just ask.

promptfun.fun is an MCP app for ChatGPT. MCP is the open standard for giving ChatGPT new abilities. There’s nothing to download and no promptfun.fun account to make.

  1. Copy the connector link

    Posted here at launch

    The public link isn’t out yet. It goes here the day it opens.

  2. Add it to ChatGPT

    On chatgpt.com, turn on developer mode under Settings → Apps → Advanced settings. Then open Plugins, choose + and Add custom MCP server, name it promptfun.fun and paste the link.

    Today ChatGPT only lets custom apps take actions in Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces, on the web. See “Which ChatGPT plans work?” below.

  3. Ask for what you want

    Start a chat and say it. ChatGPT shows you the preview, you confirm in the chat, and the receipt comes back there too.

Things to say

  • “Launch a token called Moonbeam, ticker BEAM.”
  • “What can promptfun.fun do right now?”

If something isn’t supported yet, promptfun.fun says so instead of guessing.

Safety

Nothing happens until you say so. Every time.

We never ask for your keys

Launching won’t need a wallet, and that part is being built. If you bring your own, it signs and sends the transaction itself. We never see or store your keys or seed phrase.

Nothing faked

No made-up results and no pretend success. A receipt only says “confirmed” after we read the transaction back from the chain.

You see what happens

The preview is read from the transaction itself, not from ChatGPT’s summary. What you confirm is what goes onchain.

Only you can confirm

ChatGPT can ask for an action, but only you can confirm it. Each confirmation covers one action and nothing else.

FAQ

Before you plug it into ChatGPT.

What is promptfun.fun?

It’s an app for ChatGPT that turns what you ask for into a real onchain action. It prepares the transaction, shows you a plain-language preview, lets you confirm it in the chat and hands back a receipt checked against the chain. It all happens inside ChatGPT.

Do I need to know about crypto?

No. You need a ChatGPT account. promptfun.fun explains each action in plain words before you confirm it.

Do I need a wallet?

Not to launch a token, once that part is ready. It’s being built: promptfun.fun will pay the launch network fee, and you confirm in the chat. If you’d rather use your own wallet, you can. You approve on a promptfun.fun page in your wallet, and that’s how the devnet tests run today. Before launches without a wallet open, we’ll spell out exactly where your new token goes.

Is it live?

Not yet. Token launches on Solana are being tested on devnet, which uses test tokens with no real value, with your own wallet. Confirming in chat with no wallet, with promptfun.fun paying the fee, is being built. pump.fun launches are coming, and so are Ethereum, Robinhood Chain and Base. This page says when each one opens.

Which chains does it support?

Solana comes first. Then EVM chains, in this order: Ethereum, Robinhood Chain and Base. The Chains section above shows the current state of each one.

Does promptfun.fun hold my money or keys?

It never asks for your keys or seed phrase. If you use your own wallet, your wallet signs and sends every transaction, and promptfun.fun never holds your funds.

Can ChatGPT do things without asking me?

No. ChatGPT can prepare an action, but nothing happens onchain until you confirm it in the chat, or approve it in your wallet if you use one. If you cancel, nothing is sent.

What does it cost?

Each chain charges a network fee. For token launches, promptfun.fun will pay it for you (being built). With your own wallet, your wallet pays it to the chain. Either way, the preview shows the estimate before you confirm. As of 9 Oct 2026, launching a token cost about $0.42–0.55 on Solana and $0.16–0.28 on Ethereum. See Network fees for every chain. promptfun.fun hasn’t set its own pricing.

Which ChatGPT plans work?

Today, ChatGPT lets custom MCP apps take actions, like launching a token, only in Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces, on the web, after an admin turns on developer mode. Pro accounts can connect custom apps, but only for read-only tools. Mobile isn’t supported yet. We’ll update this when ChatGPT opens it up to more plans. Source: OpenAI Help Center, checked 9 Oct 2026.

What if something goes wrong?

You get the real status, right in the chat. If the transaction failed or you cancelled it, ChatGPT tells you that. It never says something worked when it didn’t.
