Privacy policy

Last updated 9 Oct 2026. Draft for review before the public connector opens.

What we collect

When you use promptfun.fun through Claude, Anthropic processes your chat under its own privacy policy. Our MCP server receives the tool calls needed to prepare and confirm actions, such as token names, tickers, wallet addresses you provide and transaction identifiers.

Public blockchains are public. Addresses, transactions and token metadata you publish onchain are visible to anyone.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your seed phrase or private keys. When you use your own wallet, signing happens in the wallet; we do not receive your keys.

We do not sell personal information. We do not run third-party ad trackers on this marketing site.

Logs and operations

We keep short-lived server logs for reliability and abuse prevention (for example request timestamps and error codes). Operational metrics exclude secrets. Public status endpoints do not expose configuration or wallet balances.

Retention

Logs are rotated on a schedule appropriate for operations. Onchain data persists on the blockchain independently of promptfun.fun.

Your choices

You can stop using the connector at any time by turning it off in Claude. You choose what to approve in your wallet.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes before launch will be noted on the home page.

See also the Terms of use (draft).
