Solana wallet

Send trading fees to your wallet

Fee tiers (with or without $PROMPT) are on the home page. This page is only about choosing the Solana wallet that receives your share.

Two ways in Claude

  1. Say your address when you launch

    Include your Solana wallet in plain language in the same chat message as your launch request.

    Example: “Launch MOON on mainnet — send my trading fees to …” (paste your full address).

    A Solana address is usually 43–44 characters: letters and numbers only, no spaces.

  2. Claim wallet (email)

    Go to promptfun.fun/claim. Sign in with the same email you use in Claude. We link a user-owned Solana payout wallet to your account.

    If claim is unavailable, paste your own external wallet address in chat instead.
