Add to Claude

Add it once. Then just ask.

promptfun.fun is a connector for Claude, and it works on the Free plan. It uses MCP, the open standard for giving AI assistants new abilities. There’s nothing to download and no promptfun.fun account to make.

  1. Copy the connector link

    https://promptfun.fun/mcp

    Remote MCP endpoint on this site. Choose No sign-in in Claude unless your org uses OAuth.

  2. Add it to Claude

    Open in Claude

    Opens Claude’s add-connector dialog with the link above prefilled. Review it, then Continue and Add. You can also paste the link yourself: on claude.ai or in Claude Desktop, go to Customize → Connectors, choose + Add, then Add custom connector.

    The Free plan allows one custom connector. On Team and Enterprise, an Owner adds it for the organization first, then you connect it. See “Which plans work?” below.

  3. Turn it on and ask

    In a chat, open + → Connectors and switch promptfun.fun on. Then say what you want. Claude shows you the preview, you tap Launch it, and the receipt comes back in the same chat.

Safety

Nothing happens until you say so. Every time.

We never ask for your keys

Mainnet pump.fun launches use Launch it in chat — no wallet, no seed phrase. Wallet approval on the /approve page is fallback only. We never see or store your keys.

Nothing faked

No made-up results and no pretend success. A receipt only says “confirmed” after we read the transaction back from the chain.

You see what happens

The preview is read from the transaction itself, not from Claude’s summary. What you approve is what goes onchain.

Only you can approve

Claude can ask for an action, but only you can approve it. Each approval covers one action and nothing else.

FAQ

Before you connect Claude.

What is promptfun.fun?

It’s a connector for Claude that turns what you ask for into a real onchain action. It prepares the transaction, shows you a plain-language preview, lets you approve it in your wallet and hands back a receipt checked against the chain.

Do I need to know about crypto?

No. You need a Claude account, and the Free plan works. promptfun.fun explains each action in plain words before you approve it.

Do I need a wallet?

No for the default path. Solana mainnet pump.fun launches use Launch it in chat; promptfun.fun pays network fees from the sponsor wallet. You only need a wallet if you explicitly choose the approval-page fallback or for transfers on testnets.

Is it live?

Yes. Sponsored Solana mainnet (pump.fun, no wallet), Solana devnet for ops, and EVM public testnets (including Robinhood Chain Testnet) are live via the Claude connector. Mainnet uses real SOL from the sponsor wallet; testnets use test tokens only. EVM mainnets are not open yet.

Which chains does it support?

Solana comes first. Then EVM chains, in this order: Ethereum, Robinhood Chain and Base. The Chains section above shows the current state of each one.

Does promptfun.fun hold my money or keys?

It never asks for your keys or seed phrase. If you use your own wallet, your wallet signs and sends every transaction, and promptfun.fun never holds your funds.

Can Claude do things without asking me?

No. Claude can prepare an action, but nothing happens onchain until you approve it in the chat, or in your wallet if you use one. If you cancel, nothing is sent.

What does it cost?

Each chain charges a network fee. On Solana mainnet pump.fun, promptfun.fun pays real SOL from the sponsor wallet when you tap Launch it. The preview shows the estimate before you confirm. As of 9 Oct 2026, launching a token cost about $0.42–0.55 on Solana and $0.16–0.28 on Ethereum. See Network fees. promptfun.fun hasn’t set its own pricing.

Which Claude plans work?

Custom connectors work on every Claude plan: Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise. The Free plan allows one custom connector, and on Team and Enterprise an Owner adds it for the organization first. Source: Claude Help Center, checked 9 Oct 2026.

What if something goes wrong?

You get the real status, right in the chat. If the transaction failed or you cancelled it, Claude tells you that. It never says something worked when it didn’t.
